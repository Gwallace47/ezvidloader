Privacy Policy

ezvidloader · Operated by snapflic · Last updated: November 17, 2026

ezvidloader is a private tool used by its operator to publish short highlight clips from Twitch streams, credited to the streamer, to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What we access

  • Twitch: public information through the Twitch API (whether a streamer is live, the game category, and past broadcasts) for public GTA 6 streams.
  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to the operator's own channel. The app does not read, change, or delete other channel data.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar, privacy options) and permission to post videos to the operator's own TikTok account.

How we use and store it

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's computer and are used only to upload clips. Audio from past broadcasts is processed on that computer to find highlight moments and write captions, then deleted. Short transcripts of candidate moments are sent to Anthropic's API to choose clips and write titles. We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Viewers

The app collects no data about people who watch the published videos.

Third-party services

By using this app's YouTube features, the operator agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok's handling is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and TikTok access under TikTok Settings → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access or asking us to delete data removes the stored tokens within 7 days.

Streamers

If you are a streamer whose content was clipped and you want clips removed or no longer want to be clipped, email us and we will take the clips down and stop within 48 hours.
