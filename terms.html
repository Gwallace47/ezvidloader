What the app is

ezvidloader is a private tool that turns highlight moments from Twitch streams into short vertical videos and publishes them to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. Only the operator uses it; it does not post to anyone else's accounts.

Content, credit and removal

  • Clips are short highlights taken from public Twitch broadcasts of GTA 6, including creators who have given permission directly or through their own clipping program.
  • Creators can ask never to be clipped by emailing snapflic@gmail.com; they are then excluded from all future clips.
  • Every clip credits the original streamer on screen and in its description or caption.
  • Clips are removed promptly at the streamer's request.
  • Content that breaks YouTube's or TikTok's Community Guidelines is not published.
