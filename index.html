What ezvidloader does

It's a publishing tool used by the snapflic channels on TikTok and YouTube. It does the slow parts of clipping: watching hours of footage, finding the highlights, editing them to 9:16 and writing captions.

1

Finds the moments

Listens to finished GTA 6 broadcasts for spikes in excitement (big reactions, chaos, laughter) and reads what was said around them.

2

Edits the clip

Cuts a 15–60 second clip, reframes it to vertical 9:16, and adds word-by-word captions and a short hook at the top.

3

Credits and publishes

Puts the streamer's channel on screen and in the caption, then posts it to the snapflic TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Built around credit

Clips exist to send viewers back to the streamer.

  • The streamer's Twitch channel is shown on screen for the whole clip.
  • Every caption links the streamer's channel.
  • Creators can opt out with one email and are never clipped again.
  • Clips are removed within 48 hours on request.
  • Only short highlights are posted, never full streams.

Where the clips go

ezvidloader publishes only to accounts owned by its operator. It has no sign-up and never posts to anyone else's account.

TikTok

Posts to @snapflic through TikTok's official Content Posting API, after the operator logs in with Login Kit.

YouTube Shorts

Uploads to the snapflic YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Posting limits

At most 5 clips a day per platform, spaced out through the day, so feeds aren't flooded.
