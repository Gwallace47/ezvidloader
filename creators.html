For creators

If you stream GTA 6 on Twitch, your highlights might be clipped here. Here's what that means for you.

You're always credited

Your channel name (twitch.tv/you) is shown on screen for the whole clip and in the caption, so viewers can find your stream.

Want to be clipped?

Email snapflic@gmail.com with your Twitch name. You can also ask for specific hashtags or your own clipping program's rules, and we'll follow them.

Don't want to be clipped?

Email snapflic@gmail.com from an address linked to your channel, or DM @snapflic on TikTok. You'll be added to the opt-out list and never clipped again.

Want a clip taken down?

Send the link to the clip. It will be removed within 48 hours, no questions asked.

Do you make money from my clips?

The snapflic accounts may earn from platform creator programs. If you run a paid clipping program, we're happy to join it and follow its rules.

Do you use my full stream?

No. Only short highlights of 15–60 seconds are posted.

Which games?

Only Grand Theft Auto VI.
